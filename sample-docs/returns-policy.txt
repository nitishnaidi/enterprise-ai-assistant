Acme Corp Customer Returns Policy

1. Return Window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full
refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by
proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

2. Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are final sale and cannot be returned: gift cards,
downloadable software, perishable goods, and personal care items that have
been opened.

3. Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
after we receive and inspect the returned item. Shipping fees are non-refundable
unless the return is due to a defect or shipping error on our part.

4. Exchanges
We offer free exchanges for a different size or color within the same 30-day
window, subject to availability. Exchanges do not require a new payment
authorization unless the replacement item has a different price.

5. Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of
delivery. We will cover return shipping costs and offer either a full refund
or a free replacement, customer's choice.

6. How to Start a Return
Log in to your account, go to Order History, select the item, and choose
"Start a Return." Print the prepaid shipping label and drop the package off
at any authorized carrier location within 14 days of generating the label.

7. International Orders
International orders follow the same 30-day return window, but the customer
is responsible for return shipping costs and any customs fees incurred.
